1. 100% Digital Delivery Notice

At Shora Minimalist, we specialize exclusively in high-value digital lifestyle tools, printable organizers, and editable Canva templates.

NO PHYSICAL GOODS ARE SHIPPED OR POSTED. * You will not receive any physical package in the mail or courier service. * There are zero shipping fees, zero postage charges, and zero customs fees associated with your purchase.

2. How Delivery Works

  • Order Confirmation: Immediately after your payment is successfully processed via Stripe, your order is automatically generated.
  • Instant Delivery Email: An automated fulfillment email is dispatched to the email address provided during checkout within 1 to 5 minutes.
  • Contents of Delivery Email:
  • A direct download button or link for your PDF documents (e.g., ADHD-Friendly Daily Cleaning Planner*);
  • Direct access links for your Canva templates (e.g., 30 Healthy Food Swaps Editable Canva Template*);
  • * Links to bonus resources (e.g., 150+ Instagram templates and Canva mini-course guide);
  • * An itemized payment receipt.

3. Technical & System Requirements

To access, view, and edit your digital products, ensure your device meets the following basic requirements:

  • For PDF Planners: A standard PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Apple Preview, Google Drive, or any modern web browser. Planners can also be imported into digital note-taking apps like GoodNotes, Notability, or Penly on iPad and Android tablets.
  • For Canva Templates: A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access and a free account at Canva.com. Canva Pro is NOT required. For best editing ease, we recommend using a desktop or laptop web browser.

4. Troubleshooting Missing or Delayed Delivery Emails

If you have not received your delivery email within 10 minutes of completing your order, please follow these simple steps:

  • Check Spam / Junk Folders: Automated emails containing links can occasionally be redirected by strict email filters into your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
  • Verify Email Address: Double-check whether an alternative email (such as an Apple ID hide-my-email address or PayPal/Stripe account email) was used during checkout.
  • Whitelist Our Email: Add support@shoraminimalist.com to your email contacts or safe senders list.
  • Contact Us for Immediate Re-send: If your email is still nowhere to be found, email us at support@shoraminimalist.com with your name and payment confirmation. Our support team will manually re-send your download links within 24 hours.

5. File Storage & Re-download Policy

We recommend saving your downloaded PDF files and bookmarking your Canva template links to a secure folder on your computer or cloud storage (e.g. Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive) upon receipt.

If you ever lose access to your downloaded files or change computers, simply email support@shoraminimalist.com with your original purchase details, and we will happily reissue your access links free of charge.
