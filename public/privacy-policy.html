1. Who We Are & Data Controller Details

Shora Minimalist ("we", "us", "our") is the Data Controller responsible for your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

  • Website: https://shoraminimalist.com
  • Data Protection Contact: support@shoraminimalist.com

We take the privacy and security of our customers seriously and are committed to handling all personal data transparently, securely, and lawfully.

2. The Personal Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver your digital purchases, provide customer care, and maintain the functionality of our website:

  • Identity Data: Your first name and last name.
  • Contact Data: Your email address (used for delivering digital download links, receipts, and customer support).
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Order details, items purchased, timestamp, amount paid, and Stripe transaction identifiers. (Note: Full credit/debit card numbers and CVV codes are processed directly by Stripe and are never stored or seen by our servers).
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone, operating system, and anonymized interaction data collected via Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: `G-ELL8FWQ34D`).
  • Communications Data: Records of customer support correspondence sent to `support@shoraminimalist.com`.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing under UK GDPR

Under Article 6 of the UK GDPR, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing your name, email, and transaction details is necessary to deliver your purchased digital products and fulfill our sales contract with you.
  • Legal Obligation: Maintaining transaction and accounting records to comply with UK statutory financial and tax obligations (HM Revenue & Customs / HMRC regulations).
  • Legitimate Interests: Analyzing website traffic and preventing fraudulent transactions to safeguard our business and ensure optimal website performance.
  • Consent: Where you opt into email newsletters or non-essential analytical cookies (which you can withdraw at any time).

4. Third-Party Data Processors

To provide our digital e-commerce services, we partner with reputable third-party service providers who act as data processors:

  • Stripe Inc. (Payment Processing): Processes payment information securely under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.
  • Vercel Inc. (Hosting & Cloud Infrastructure): Hosts our web application and provides secure serverless edge hosting.
  • Google LLC (Google Analytics 4): Measures website usage and traffic patterns using anonymized identifiers.
  • Canva Pty Ltd (Template Delivery): Where customers click external links to open editable Canva templates on Canva's platform.

All processors are bound by strict Data Processing Agreements ensuring compliance with UK data protection standards.

5. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers (such as Stripe or Google) may process data in servers located outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), such as the United States.

Whenever we transfer your personal data outside the UK, we ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded by ensuring at least one of the following safeguards is implemented: * The country has been deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection by the UK Government; or * We use specific contracts approved for use in the UK (the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses) which give personal data the same protection it has in the UK.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Sales & Tax Records: In accordance with UK statutory accounting regulations (HMRC requirements), we retain financial transaction records for 6 years from the end of the last company financial year.
  • Customer Support Records: Emails and inquiries sent to our support desk are retained for up to 2 years to ensure continuity of customer service.
  • Analytics Data: Google Analytics data is retained according to default aggregated retention periods.

7. Your Rights under the UK GDPR

Under UK data protection law, you have enforceable legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access (Subject Access Request): You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request that we delete your personal data where there is no good reason for us continuing to process it (subject to statutory tax retention obligations).
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we suspend processing your data in certain scenarios.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request the transfer of your personal data to you or a third party in a structured format.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to processing where we are relying on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@shoraminimalist.com. We respond to all legitimate requests within one calendar month free of charge.

8. Right to Complain to the Information Commissioner (ICO)

If you are unhappy with the way we have handled your personal data, you have the right to make a complaint at any time to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection issues:

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113
  • Address: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concerns before you approach the ICO, so please contact us first at support@shoraminimalist.com.
