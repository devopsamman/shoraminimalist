1. Overview and Scope of Products

This Return and Refund Policy applies to all digital goods, downloadable planners, printable templates, and editable Canva eBook templates purchased through Shora Minimalist (trading at shoraminimalist.com).

Because our products (including the ADHD-Friendly Daily Cleaning Planner and Done For You Guide on 30 Healthy Food Swaps Canva Template) are delivered electronically and immediately upon purchase, specific statutory rules apply under UK law regarding refunds and cancellations.

2. The UK 14-Day Cooling-Off Period & Statutory Waiver

Under the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers ordinarily have a statutory right to cancel distance contracts within 14 calendar days without giving any reason.

However, Regulation 37(1) provides a specific exception for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium: * When you purchase a digital product from Shora Minimalist, you expressly request and consent to immediate digital delivery (either via instant download links or access to Canva template files). * You acknowledge that once access or download begins, your statutory 14-day right of cancellation is waived and expires immediately.

By completing checkout on shoraminimalist.com, you confirm: 1. You consent to the immediate supply of the digital content. 2. You acknowledge and accept that your statutory right to cancel the contract under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 is lost once digital delivery commences.

Important Notice for UK Consumers

Due to the non-returnable nature of immediately downloadable files, we cannot offer change-of-mind refunds once files or template links have been accessed, unless the digital product is faulty or not as described.

3. Your Rights for Faulty or Damaged Digital Content (Consumer Rights Act 2015)

Your statutory rights under the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 are fully protected. Under the Act, all digital content supplied to you must be: * Of satisfactory quality (free from corrupt data, malware, or technical defects); * Fit for a particular purpose (functions as reasonable expectations dictate); and * As described (matches the product description, sample images, and specifications provided on our website).

Your Remedies If Digital Content Is Faulty: 1. **Right to Repair or Replacement:** If your downloaded PDF is corrupt, a template link is broken, or a file does not function as described, contact us immediately. We will repair the file or provide a replacement working version within 24–48 hours free of charge and without significant inconvenience. 2. **Right to a Price Reduction or Full Refund:** If we are unable to repair or replace the faulty digital content within a reasonable time, or if the fault cannot be remedied without significant inconvenience to you, you are legally entitled to a price reduction or a full 100% refund of the purchase price paid.

4. Eligible Circumstances for Refunds

We will promptly grant a full refund under the following verified circumstances: * Defective or Corrupted Files: If a digital file cannot be opened or accessed and our support team cannot provide a working replacement within 48 hours. * Material Misrepresentation: If the digital product received is materially different from what was described on the product page. * Accidental Duplicate Purchase: If you mistakenly purchased the exact same digital product twice in error within a 24-hour window, we will gladly refund the duplicate transaction. * Unfulfilled Delivery: If technical failure prevented the delivery email from reaching you, and we are unable to deliver the files to an alternative email address provided by you.

5. Ineligible Circumstances

We cannot approve refunds in the following scenarios: * Change of Mind: Deciding you no longer need the planner or template after the download link has already been opened or accessed. * Software Incompatibility Due to Neglect of Stated Requirements: We explicitly state that Canva templates require a free Canva account (at canva.com) and PDF planners require a standard PDF viewer (e.g. Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, or browser). Inability to open files due to refusal to use compatible free software does not constitute a product defect. * Personal Preference: Minor stylistic disagreements (e.g. preferring a different font or color tone) when the product accurately matches the screenshots and descriptions displayed on our site. * Unauthorized Resale or Distribution: Breach of license terms (such as reselling original editable links).

6. How to Request a Refund or Technical Assistance

If you experience any issue with your purchase, please submit a request to our UK support team by following these steps:

  • Email: Send a message to support@shoraminimalist.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Order ID]".
  • Details Required: Include your full name, the email address used at checkout, your Stripe receipt number or order reference, and a concise description of the problem.
  • Screenshots/Evidence: If claiming a technical defect or broken link, please attach a screenshot or screen recording demonstrating the error.
  • Assessment & Resolution: Our team investigates every request within 1–2 business days. If technical remediation fails, your refund will be authorized immediately.

7. Processing Timeframes and Method of Payment

Once a refund is approved by Shora Minimalist: * The refund will be credited back directly to the original payment method used at checkout (via Stripe). * For security and anti-money laundering compliance, refunds cannot be redirected to a different card or account. * Bank Processing Times: Stripe processes the reversal immediately; depending on your bank or card issuer (e.g., Barclays, HSBC, Monzo, Revolut, NatWest), funds typically appear in your account within 5 to 10 business days. * You will receive an automated Stripe refund notification email once the reversal is triggered.

8. Legal Disclaimer & Consumer Protection

Nothing in this Return and Refund Policy is intended to limit, exclude, or affect your statutory consumer rights under the laws of England and Wales, Scotland, or Northern Ireland. For independent advice about your legal rights as a consumer in the United Kingdom, you may contact the Citizens Advice Consumer Service at citizensadvice.org.uk or call 0808 223 1133.
